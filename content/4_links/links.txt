secondaryBox: 

[RSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSS), or Really Simple Syndication, is a great way to keep up with news and information from around the internet. [NetNewWire](https://netnewswire.com) is my RSS reader of choice. I see it as a way to keep the flow information distributed rather than concentrating in the hands of a handful of social media companies.
